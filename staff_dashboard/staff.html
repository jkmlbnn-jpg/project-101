About EquipTrack

EquipTrack is a web-based system designed to make equipment borrowing within the College of Informatics and Computing Sciences (CICS) easier and more organized.

Students and faculty can browse available equipment, submit borrowing requests, and monitor the status of their applications.

Borrowing Rules and Guidelines

Return Equipment on Time

Make sure to return borrowed equipment on or before the scheduled return date.

Handle Equipment with Care

Use the equipment responsibly and avoid causing damage.

Provide Accurate Information

Always provide correct personal details when submitting a borrowing request.

Wait for Staff Approval

Do not assume your request is approved until the equipment staff confirms it.
